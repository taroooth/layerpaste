LayerPaste is a Chrome extension that copies a web page into Figma as editable layers. The free version allows 3 copies per day. LayerPaste Pro is a one-time purchase of USD 9.99 that unlocks unlimited copies and full-page capture. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.
